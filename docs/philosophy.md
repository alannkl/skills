# Philosophy

Beliefs about engineering with AI, earned from daily work rather than borrowed. The list grows slowly.

## The human stays load-bearing

_Intent lives in humans._

AI will probably solve coding. Engineering is a different bet, because the hardest bug was never in the code. It's in what humans thought they had explained clearly.

We don't know what we want until we see something that isn't it. Requirements are discovered through iteration. That's not a communication failure to fix. It's how humans figure out their own intent.

The human isn't an imperfect spec-writer to be routed around. They're a load-bearing part of the loop, the only source of "that's not what I meant."

And that's the easy case: one human. Real engineering answers to many. Business, org, team, each holding a different piece of the intent, some of it contradictory. With every person added, the challenge doesn't add up. It multiplies.

The job title might change. The human stays.

## Attention is the scarce resource

_For humans and models alike. More input degrades focus and understanding._
