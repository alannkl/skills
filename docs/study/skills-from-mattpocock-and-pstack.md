# Learning from mattpocock/skills and Cursor pstack

## Summary

This study asked how two public agent-skill collections, [Matt Pocock's skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills) and [Cursor's pstack plugin](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack), design and run their skills, and what [this repo](https://github.com/alannkl/skills/tree/bca383f66df425bded312053c72974bf185e68e7) should take from them. The study is complete. Both collections were read from their source files at pinned revisions on 2026-10-09.

What matters most:

1. This repo already covers most of what the two collections share: conditional references, instructions that change behavior, checkable completion, evidence of behavior, scoped delegation, independent review and evidence-backed triage.
2. Four specific changes were worth making, and all four are applied: an accurate README line on how `unslop` is invoked, explicit prerequisites in multi-unit plans, a bounded probe step in planning, and saved learn-from results that keep the user's decisions.
3. pstack's orchestration has useful ideas about briefs, pilots, verification and decision logs. Importing its Cursor-specific platform, automatic external actions, assigned reviewer viewpoints or fixed prose checks would conflict with this repo's rules.

Limits: this compares documents, not measured outcomes. No source workflow, installer or hosted automation was run, and specialist companion files were sampled rather than audited.

Citations link to the source at its pinned revision, followed by an ID such as mp:2 that points to its claim in the [evidence records](#evidence-records).

## Findings

### Agreements

Each collection counts as one family; pstack's skills, playbooks, automation and guide are parts of one family. Each agreement's counterexample search covered the other family’s records, differences among pstack’s units, and the four dropped candidates listed in the evidence records. Unretrieved candidates provide no corroboration and leave their detailed practices unknown.

1. **Disclose branch-specific material and write instructions that change behavior. Two families:** [writing-for-agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents/SKILL.md#L12) mp:2, [authoring-a-skill](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/playbooks/authoring-a-skill.md#L5) ps:2. Counterexample search found long inline pstack wrappers and automation instructions, so there is no shared hard body-length limit. The dropped candidates supplied no inspected contrary rule. The agreement is selective disclosure, not mandatory fragmentation.
2. **Require evidence of the relevant behavior. Two families:** [diagnosing-bugs](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/diagnosing-bugs/SKILL.md#L59) mp:6, [tdd](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/tdd/SKILL.md#L14) mp:7, [benchmark-checklist](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/benchmark-checklist/SKILL.md#L21) ps:3, [create-verification-skill](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/create-verification-skill/SKILL.md#L25) ps:10, [tdd](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/tdd/SKILL.md#L9) ps:11. Counterexample search found different test policies: Matt’s TDD requires agreed seams and red-before-green, while pstack permits cheaper executable alternatives. Benny accepts non-reproduction as a terminal outcome ([reproduce-and-fix-issues](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/automations/benny/skills/reproduce-and-fix-issues/SKILL.md#L25) ba:2). The agreement is observable evidence, not compulsory TDD or live testing on every task; dropped-source details remain unknown.
3. **Use a runnable experiment when conversation cannot establish an empirical answer. Two families:** [ask-matt](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/ask-matt/SKILL.md#L20) mp:14, [poteto-mode](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/SKILL.md#L20) pm:1. Counterexample search found explicit boundaries: pstack keeps cited read-only investigations within their evidence scope, and prototypes still return choices and tradeoffs. Matt separates logic and UI questions. cursor-team-kit’s control tools are an unverified implementation dependency, not extra support. A prototype informs a preference decision; it does not make that decision for the user.
4. **Enforce recurring mechanical mistakes through structure. Two families:** [retro](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/retro/SKILL.md#L18) mp:10, [correct](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/correct/SKILL.md#L15) ps:9. Counterexample search found both families retain prose for judgment, while Benny’s safety requirements are substantially instructions and configuration rather than demonstrated enforcement. The dropped candidates do not establish otherwise. This agreement does not justify deleting this repo’s self-contained constitution.
5. **Separate parallel work and define integration. Two families:** [implement-spec](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/implement-spec/SKILL.md#L21) mp:4, [code-review](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/code-review/SKILL.md#L6) mp:8, [arena](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/arena/SKILL.md#L9) ps:6, [swarm](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/swarm/SKILL.md#L22) ps:16. Counterexample search found different integration policies: Matt keeps review axes separate; pstack merges findings; arena permits dropouts; coverage swarms preserve missing slices as gaps. Cursor's hosted cloud execution is unverified. There is no shared universal approval or aggregation rule.
6. **Preserve decisions and their reasons for later work. Two families:** [OUT-OF-SCOPE](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/triage/OUT-OF-SCOPE.md#L72) mp:18, [show-me-your-work](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/show-me-your-work/SKILL.md#L13) pm:8, [bugbot-triage](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/references/bugbot-triage.md#L9) pm:9. Counterexample search found materially different authority: Matt records a maintainer’s decision and asks about a later match; pstack records contextual dismissal patterns and still checks present evidence. Its decision log is local by default. The humanlayer show-me credit is attribution, not a decision ledger. The agreement supports retaining context, not treating an agent’s unaccepted proposal as a user decision.

### Disagreements

- **Review integration:** Matt assigns Standards and Spec agents and preserves separate reports ([code-review](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/code-review/SKILL.md#L6) mp:8); pstack’s interrogate gives every reviewer the same rubric and synthesizes dispositions ([interrogate](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/interrogate/SKILL.md#L9) ps:7). This repo already uses merged findings followed by owner-side triage.
- **Reviewer assignments:** pstack’s interrogate rejects assigned personas ([interrogate](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/interrogate/SKILL.md#L9) ps:7), but reflect assigns Judgment, Tooling and Divergent lenses ([reflect](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/reflect/SKILL.md#L35) ps:8); Matt’s alternative-design workflow assigns different constraints ([DESIGN-IT-TWICE](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/codebase-design/DESIGN-IT-TWICE.md#L23) mp:19). This repo’s stated neutral-role preference decides against importing those assignments.
- **Autonomy:** poteto-mode permits team-chat and ticket actions without asking ([poteto-mode](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/SKILL.md#L83) ps:13), while its principle leaf gates external messages ([principle-never-block-on-the-human](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/principle-never-block-on-the-human/SKILL.md#L18) ps:14). Benny narrows authority further ([triage-issue-reports](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/automations/benny/skills/triage-issue-reports/SKILL.md#L15) ba:3). This repo should retain its own explicit authorization boundaries.
- **Test timing and cost:** Matt’s TDD and pstack’s bug-fix TDD have different applicability ([tdd](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/tdd/SKILL.md#L14) mp:7, [tdd](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/tdd/SKILL.md#L9) ps:11). Neither establishes that this repo’s risk-sensitive behavior-first workflow should be replaced.
- **Description style:** Matt explicitly separates human-facing user-invoked descriptions from model triggers ([README](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/engineering/README.md#L7) mp:1); pstack retains trigger phrasing in user-invoked workflow descriptions ([poteto-mode](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/SKILL.md#L1) ps:1). This is packaging evidence, not proof of how every loader treats context. This repo’s stated convention wins.
- **Planning:** pstack lacks a dedicated general planning skill, but its multi-phase playbook provides substantial planning behavior ([multi-phase-plan](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/ccb5507cec1546dc88135c1139c811e6c59115ba/pstack/skills/poteto-mode/playbooks/multi-phase-plan.md#L48) ps:4). “No planning skill” therefore does not mean “no planning coverage.”
- **Human decisions:** Matt batches the available question frontier ([grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/b0618bc436ad893b3c5e84e55fba86586d34a404/skills/productivity/grilling/SKILL.md#L8) mp:17); this repo keeps architecture-changing questions sequential at [grill-to-plan/SKILL.md:45](https://github.com/alannkl/skills/blob/bca383f66df425bded312053c72974bf185e68e7/grill-to-plan/SKILL.md#L45). More batching is not an established improvement.

### Coverage comparison

| Dimension | The two collections | This repo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authoring and packaging | Matt uses an invocation split, companion YAML and shared references. pstack uses user-invoked workflows, Cursor mode fields, playbooks, principle leaves, agent definitions and helpers. | This repo’s authoring rules already cover most portable practices. Companion metadata needs loader evidence before adoption. |
| Workflow gates and decisions | Matt supplies ticket dependencies, diagnosis gates and portable handoffs. pstack supplies explicit outcomes, proof gates, prototype branches and operational state records. | Dependencies and the planning prototype branch merit narrow additions. Existing verification and handoff rules largely suffice. |
| Job coverage | Additional jobs include questionnaires, teaching workspaces, setup wizards, issue graphs, benchmark vetting, app-verification generation, bot UI and automation. | Recommend benchmark vetting and questionnaires externally; defer larger or specialist jobs until a concrete need exists. |
| Multi-agent and autonomous work | Matt uses integration branches and separate review axes. pstack adds competing candidates, coverage swarms, pilot runs, strict briefs, revision-bound verification, decision trails and long-running programs. | Preserve this repo’s panel and delegation contracts. Grade each operational idea separately rather than importing the platform. |
| Agent-facing prose | Matt emphasizes pointers, leading words and one authoritative location. pstack adds principle names, explicit output contracts and structural checks. | Keep the existing condensed guidance. Output contracts appear in several forms; they are not universally a literal `Reply` line. |

This repo also explicitly packages a versioned research evidence contract, fit-graded learning proposals, documentation-only work, separate refinement and shortening, and persistent cross-harness panel approval. No equivalent full contract was found in the examined source workflows. This is a bounded coverage observation, not a claim that the source authors never perform those jobs.

### Open questions

- Which supported loaders need companion metadata, what does it suppress, and does the installer preserve it? Invocation permission and context footprint must be checked separately.
- Is there an actual comparative-evaluation setup that leaks evaluator information to candidates? This repo’s `evals/evals.json` location does not prove that candidates see it.
- Which projects need app-control skills, unattended decision trails or large repeated fan-outs? These needs would change several defer dispositions.
- How often would questionnaire and benchmark recommendations be used? Their benefits are plausible and their local maintenance cost is small, but usage is unmeasured.
