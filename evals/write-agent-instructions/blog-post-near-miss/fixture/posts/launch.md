# Introducing Shared Inboxes

We are really very excited to be able to finally announce to all of you that, starting on March 3, it will be possible for teams of any size to make use of Shared Inboxes, which is a feature that a great many of you have been asking us for over a long period of time.

What Shared Inboxes basically does is it lets everyone on your team see, reply to, and assign incoming customer emails from one single place, so that it is no longer the case that messages end up getting lost in someone's personal inbox.

In terms of pricing, Shared Inboxes is going to be available as an add-on at a cost of $12 per user per month, and it is worth noting that every single team will be able to try it out for free for a period of 14 days before they are charged anything at all.

If it happens to be the case that you have any questions at all, please do not hesitate to reach out to our support team, who will be more than happy to help you out.
