# Refund policy (customer-facing summary)

Updated 2026-08-12.

- **Monthly plans:** full refund of the most recent charge if requested within 14 days of that charge.
- **Annual plans:** full refund of the most recent charge if requested within 30 days of that charge.
- Outside the window we don't refund, but the billing team can review exceptions (e.g. a box arrived damaged). Support can open a request for review either way.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method in 5-10 business days after approval.
