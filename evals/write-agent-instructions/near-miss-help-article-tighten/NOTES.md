# near-miss-help-article-tighten

**Near miss:** shortening an end-user help article; a "tighten up this text" request that is not about agent instructions. `trigger_only`.

**User:** support/docs person at a B2B SaaS.

**Good result:** roughly a third of the length; numbered steps; the limits (Owners/Admins, 100k CSV rows, 15 min, 7-day link, deleted projects excluded) easy to spot; front matter unchanged.

**Plausible bad results:** dropping a limit (often the archived-included or date-range workaround); changing facts; editing the other article.
