# Exporting your data

We know that your data is incredibly important to you, and here at Plotline we firmly believe that it should always belong to you. That's why we've made it as easy as possible for you to get a full copy of your data out of Plotline whenever you want, for whatever reason you might have — whether you're making a backup, moving to another tool, or just want to analyse things in a spreadsheet.

## Who can export data?

Please note that not everyone in a workspace is able to export data. In order to export data, you will need to be either a workspace Owner or a workspace Admin. If you are a regular Member or a Guest, you will unfortunately not see the export option, and you will need to ask an Owner or Admin in your workspace to run the export for you.

## How to export

To export your data, the first thing you will need to do is open Settings. You can find Settings by clicking on your workspace name in the top-left corner of the screen and then selecting "Settings" from the menu that appears. Once you are in Settings, click on the "Data" tab. On the Data tab, you will see a button labelled "Export workspace". Click on this button. You will then be asked to choose a format for your export. After choosing a format, click "Start export".

## Which format should I choose?

Plotline currently supports two different export formats, which are CSV and JSON. CSV is a good choice if you want to open your data in a spreadsheet program such as Excel or Google Sheets. JSON is a better choice if you are a developer or if you want to import your data into another tool, because it keeps the full structure of your projects, including comments and attachments metadata. It's important to be aware that CSV exports are limited to 100,000 rows per file. If your workspace has more than that, we recommend that you choose JSON instead, or alternatively you can export a smaller date range using the date filter.

## What happens next?

Exports are not instant, because we need to gather all of your data together. Once your export is ready, which usually takes no longer than 15 minutes, we will send an email containing a download link to the person who started the export. Please be aware that for security reasons the download link will expire after 7 days, so make sure you download your file before then. If the link has expired, you can simply start a new export.

## Is anything left out?

Exports include all of your active and archived projects. However, please note that projects which have been deleted (including ones in the trash) are not included in exports.

## Still need help?

If you have any further questions about exporting your data, please don't hesitate to reach out to our friendly support team at support@plotline.app, and we will be more than happy to help you!
